AYURVEDA DIET KNOWLEDGE BASE (FOR RAG)

CORE CONCEPTS Panchamahabhuta (5 Elements): Akasha (Ether), Vayu (Air),
Agni (Fire), Jala (Water), Prithvi (Earth)

Tridosha: Vata = Air + Ether Pitta = Fire + Water Kapha = Water + Earth

Health = balance of doshas Disease = imbalance

AGNI (DIGESTIVE FIRE) Types: Sama Agni (balanced) Vishama Agni
(irregular, Vata) Tikshna Agni (sharp, Pitta) Manda Agni (slow, Kapha)

Rules: Eat only after previous meal digests Avoid overeating Warm food
improves digestion Cold food weakens digestion

AMA (TOXINS) Caused by: Overeating, weak digestion, incompatible foods

Signs: Coated tongue, lethargy, bloating

Diet: Light, warm, spiced foods

SHADRASA (6 TASTES) Sweet, Sour, Salty, Pungent, Bitter, Astringent

VATA DIET Qualities: cold, dry, light Eat: warm, oily, grounding Foods:
rice, wheat, oats, cooked vegetables, milk, ghee Spices: ginger, cumin,
cinnamon Avoid: raw food, cold drinks

PITTA DIET Qualities: hot, sharp Eat: cooling, mild foods Foods: rice,
cucumber, coconut, sweet fruits Spices: fennel, coriander Avoid: chili,
fried food

KAPHA DIET Qualities: heavy, slow Eat: light, warm, dry Foods: barley,
millet, legumes, greens Spices: black pepper, turmeric Avoid: dairy,
sugar, fried food

DINACHARYA Morning: light meal Afternoon: main meal (strongest
digestion) Evening: light meal

RITUCHARYA Summer: cooling foods Winter: heavy nourishing foods Monsoon:
light, warm foods

VIRUDDHA AHARA (INCOMPATIBLE) Milk + fish Milk + sour fruits Fruit +
dairy Heated honey Equal ghee + honey

MEAL RULES 50% solids, 25% liquid, 25% empty stomach

HYDRATION RULES Sip warm water throughout the day Vata: highest
hydration (warm, slightly oily soups) Pitta: moderate hydration (cooling
liquids like coconut water) Kapha: lowest hydration (warm, spiced water)

MEAL ROTATION Rotate grains: rice, wheat, millet, barley, oats Rotate
legumes: moong, masoor, chana, toor Avoid repeating same grain more than
twice weekly

WEIGHT GOALS Brimhana (Weight Gain): Larger portions Focus on sweet and
salty tastes Add snacks: soaked almonds, dates, milk preparations

Langhana (Fat Loss): Focus on bitter, pungent, astringent tastes Very
light breakfast or skip Main meal at noon Light dinner

INGREDIENT LISTS

Grains: Basmati Rice, Brown Rice, Quinoa, Barley, Oats, Millet,
Buckwheat, Amaranth

Vegetables: Zucchini, Sweet Potato, Asparagus, Carrots, Spinach,
Cauliflower, Broccoli, Beetroot

Legumes: Moong Dal, Masoor Dal, Chana Dal, Toor Dal, Chickpeas, Lentils

Fruits: Apple, Banana, Mango, Grapes, Pomegranate, Dates, Figs

SPICES Ginger: digestion Cumin: bloating relief Fennel: cooling
Turmeric: anti-inflammatory Black pepper: metabolism booster Hing:
reduces gas

PRAKRITI VS VIKRITI Prakriti = natural constitution Vikriti = current
imbalance

MEAT (MAMSA) General: Heavy, nourishing, strength-giving Requires strong
digestion

CHICKEN Strength building Good for Vata Moderate for Pitta Limited for
Kapha

MEAT RULES Best as soup (Mamsa Rasa) Use digestive spices (ginger,
pepper, cumin) Avoid fried Avoid night consumption

INCOMPATIBLE WITH MEAT Meat + milk Meat + yogurt Meat with weak
digestion

GENERAL RULES Eat when hungry Avoid overeating Eat calmly Avoid
distractions Chew properly Do not eat late at night
